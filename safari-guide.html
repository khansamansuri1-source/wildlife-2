Gir National Park

A practical guide for your safari stay

Gir National Park is renowned as the landscape of the Asiatic lion and is one of Gujarat's most important wildlife destinations. Wildlife Gir Resort helps guests plan a comfortable resort near Gir National Park stay around official safari schedules.

  • Official booking is online through Gujarat forest systems
  • Gir Jungle Safari generally closes from 16 June to 15 October
  • Winter is a preferred season, with November to March popular for visits
Safari Timings

Plan around seasonal safari slots

Timings can change by forest department notice, so confirm while booking. Common official windows include morning, mid-morning, and evening safari slots.

Winter

16 October to 28/29 February: typically 6:30 AM to 9:30 AM, 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM, and 3:00 PM to 6:00 PM.

Summer

1 March to 15 June: typically 6:00 AM to 9:00 AM, 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM, and 4:00 PM to 7:00 PM.

Booking Notes

Carry valid ID proof, arrive early, and book permits in advance through the official Gir online permit system.

Travel Tips & FAQ

Before you visit Gir

Best Season

November to March is comfortable for families. Summer can offer strong wildlife activity, but days are hotter.

Travel Tips

  • Book safari permits before peak travel dates.
  • Carry original ID proof used for booking.
  • Wear earthy, comfortable clothing.
  • Respect forest rules and guide instructions.

FAQ

  • Is safari guaranteed? Sightings depend on nature.
  • Can the resort book permits? Our team can guide you to official booking steps.
  • Is it family friendly? Yes, with planning and early starts.
